PinPoint Support

PinPoint is recruiting software for college wrestling coaches — recruit boards, scholarship tracking, film links, and live national rankings, synced across your staff.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: email zacherlinvestments@gmail.com. I'm a college wrestling coach and the developer, so replies may take a day or two during the season.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't I see my staff's updates?

PinPoint syncs through iCloud. Make sure you're signed into iCloud on your device (Settings → your name) and that you have an internet connection. Changes made by other coaches can take a moment to appear — pull down to refresh.

How do national rankings work?

Roster rankings are pulled from publicly available WrestleStat rankings pages. Wrestlers without a current national rank show as unranked. Rankings update when you sync from the roster screen.

How do I add a recruit or wrestler?

From the Recruiting or Roster tab, tap the add button. Recruits start in the bull pen until you assign them to a coach on your staff.

Is my recruiting data private?

Yes. Your data is stored in your team's iCloud database and is only visible to your staff. It is never sold or shared with third parties. See the PinPoint Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual recruits and wrestlers directly in the app. To delete your team's data entirely, email zacherlinvestments@gmail.com and we will remove it.
