PinPoint: Recruiting

Recruiting software built by a college wrestling coach, for college wrestling coaches. No spreadsheets, no scattered text threads, no clunky web portal — your entire recruiting operation lives in your pocket and stays in sync across your whole staff.

Download on the App Store Support
Why I built it

I coach college wrestling. Our recruiting board was a spreadsheet, a text thread, and a desktop portal nobody opened on the road. I wanted the whole thing in my pocket.

Who it helps

College wrestling staffs — head coaches managing a class and budget, and assistants working their own recruits.

Core value

Every recruit, every dollar, and every film link in one place the whole staff can see — before the next call, not after.

What makes it useful

01

Run your board. Weight, class, star rating, school, contacts, and references on every recruit. Assign recruits to coaches or leave them in the bull pen, and mark commits as your class comes together.

02

Track every dollar. Offers with athletic and housing money broken out, a scholarship budget by weight class, and per-coach scoreboards with dollar-weighted rank.

03

Film and rankings at your fingertips. Video links organized by highlights, best wins, and tough losses, plus a full roster with national rankings synced from WrestleStat.

Always in sync

PinPoint syncs through iCloud. When one coach updates an offer or drops a note, the whole staff sees it. No exports, no emailed spreadsheets, no version confusion.

Pricing & compatibility

Free to download, with a yearly PinPoint membership as an in-app subscription. Requires iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
